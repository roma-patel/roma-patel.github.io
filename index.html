Roma Patel

I am a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind working on AI safety and alignment , with a particular interest in how increasingly capable language-model systems can be evaluated, aligned, and stress-tested before their failures become consequential.

My research asks how we can understand failures in language models, improve alignment objectives and training methods, and build systems that proactively surface emerging risks. I did a PhD in CS/NLP at Brown University (advised by Ellie Pavlick), and wrote my thesis on language grounding, representation, and interpretability: foundations that continue to shape how I think about what models understand and how their behavior can be made legible.

I am broadly interested in the intersection of language, reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, and long-term AI safety. Outside of work, I enjoy reading vast amounts of literature, going to new mountains, and to coffeeshops. Feel free to reach out for any research related questions!

Research Agenda

My current work is organized around three connected questions:

Understanding and interpreting models

How can we better evaluate and understand the representations and behaviors that emerge in language models?

Representative work Mapping Language Models to Grounded Conceptual Spaces ↗

Improving alignment

How can we design learning objectives and feedback mechanisms that produce robust behavior when human values are diverse, conflicting, or underspecified?

Representative work Detecting and Controlling Sycophancy with Cascading Linear Features ↗

Anticipating emerging risks

How can models and multi-agent systems help us discover failure modes that static evaluations and human red teams have not yet anticipated?
